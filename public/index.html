About

I'm a CS junior at Virginia Tech, pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science (expected May 2027). Dean's List. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering, national security, and the systems that keep critical infrastructure running.

Last fall I researched AI-driven condition-based maintenance for naval systems at the Naval Surface Warfare Center Philadelphia — watching engineers wait for failure instead of predicting it is what drives most of what I build. Currently I'm interning at Athene on the Cybersecurity / SOC team, and serving as intern lead for a 32-person intern cohort.

My long-term goal is to build a defense technology company. I'm interested in the gap between the pace of commercial AI and the speed at which the systems protecting people can absorb it.

Outside of work: I compete in debate, run long distances, and lift. I speak English, Hindi, Telugu, and Spanish.
